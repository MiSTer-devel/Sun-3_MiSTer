# SCSI bus reset recovery: 3.0.1's si_reset (0x0fef915e) asserts RST in the
# 5380's ICR (`moveb #0x80,%a1@(1)'), clears ICR, TCR and MR, and then waits
# five seconds for the targets to come back.  3.0.1 has no DELAY count here
# (1.9's movel #160000000 at 0x0fefa468): it waits on the monitor's
# millisecond clock instead, `movel 0xe0c2,%d7; addil #5000,%d7' and a
# loop until 0xe0c2 passes it (0x0fef9188-0x0fef9196; 0xe0c2 gains 10 at
# each 100 Hz tick, 0x0fef03ea).  Every disk and tape boot runs it once:
# 5 s simulated, about 25 minutes of tb_emu.  The disk and tape targets here
# are ready at once: 5000 -> 10 ms (measured 9.5 ms in tb_emu).
#
# The other waits on that clock in the sd/st drivers are taken only when a
# target is not ready, and tb_emu's never take them, so they are left
# alone: stopen's retry after a failed TEST UNIT READY (#2200 at
# 0x0fef91f8, up to four times), #2000 at 0x0fef8816 and at 0x0fef9508,
# and the disk spin-up wait (#60000 at 0x0fef70bc, "Waiting for disk to
# spin up").
